1. Remove "Free Plan" Usage Bar at Top of Prompt Base Current behavior: A bar at the top of the Prompt Base reads: "Free Plan - First five prompts unlocked. Upgrade for full access. Upgrade." Requested change: Remove this entire row. Rationale: Same logic as removing the transcript counter from the sidebar. We do not want free users knowing how many prompts they have left. Let them use the prompts until they hit the limit organically, then prompt them to upgrade. Showing "first five prompts unlocked" encourages rationing and kills momentum. Acceptance criteria: The "Free Plan" usage bar is completely removed for free/guest users No indication of how many prompts remain or how many are available on Pro Paid users are unaffected 2. Show ALL Prompts to Free Users (Remove "10 More Prompts Available on Pro") Current behavior: Below the visible prompts, there's a message: "10 more prompts available on Pro." Requested change: Remove this message. Show every single prompt to free users, no gating on visibility. Where to gate instead: When a free user clicks Copy, Download Skill, or Download on a prompt (after they've used their free allotment), that's when the upgrade prompt appears: "This is a Pro feature. Sign up to get access." Rationale: The goal is not to make users feel like they're missing out because they can't see more. The goal is to make them feel like they're missing out because they can see everything, want it, but can't act on it. Reference: PromptBase and God of Prompts use this exact pattern. They show a preview of everything, but the actual core content (the meat and potatoes) is gated behind the paywall. Acceptance criteria: All prompts are visible to free users (no "X more on Pro" messaging) Free users can browse, scroll through, and preview all prompts Upgrade prompt triggers on action (copy/download) after free limit is reached Free users should be able to see the full prompt text as a preview 3.
